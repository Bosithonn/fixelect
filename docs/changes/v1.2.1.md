### What's new in 1.2.1
- **New quick-action shortcut.** The menu now opens with **double-tap Shift** on Windows and **⌃ ⇧ Space** on a Mac. The 1.2.0 shortcuts clashed with other apps: Ctrl + Alt + Space is the Claude app's shortcut on Windows, and ⌃ ⌥ Space switches the keyboard language on a Mac. If you had the old shortcut, Fixelect moves you to the new one automatically.
- **Choose your own.** Settings → Actions has a shortcut recorder and a Reset button. On Windows it tells you when another app already uses the shortcut you picked.
- Typing capitals or Shift-clicking a selection never opens the menu: a key or a click between the two taps cancels them.
