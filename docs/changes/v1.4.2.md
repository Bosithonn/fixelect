### What's new in 1.4.2
- **Faster, especially without a graphics card.**
  - A fix right after a Polish no longer stalls: it used to take 5 seconds or more on a laptop processor (and up to 13 seconds in some cases), now about 1 second.
  - After a break, when Fixelect has freed the model's memory, the first fix is ready in about half the time. Fixelect now keeps its own instructions ready on disk, so it doesn't read them all again every time the model loads.
  - Everyday fixes are quicker too: up to a third faster on a processor, with less waiting around every fix.
- **Translate to Uzbek (beta).** Uzbek is always listed under Translate. It needs the Gemma 4 E2B model; with another model, Fixelect tells you how to switch.

### Fixes
- **Translating into Spanish, Italian and French failed** with *"Couldn't translate this"* even though the translation was fine. Fixed, and texts that the AI used to hand back untranslated (for example from Spanish or Uzbek) are now translated.
- Translations, Polish and your own actions no longer drop quotation marks you typed.
- Choosing a model from the tray menu now really switches to it.
- If the Polish preview failed to open, every shortcut stopped working until Fixelect was restarted. Fixed.
- Uninstalling now also removes your History (your recent changes) and Fixelect's other data. Your downloaded models are still only removed if you say so.
- History shows the right app for quick actions.
- Closing Settings while a model downloads still switches to it when the download finishes.
- **Mac:** the quick-action menu closes when you click somewhere else (it used to keep catching your typing), a large image on the clipboard no longer delays the shortcut, and Gemma 4 can no longer be mixed up with a small Qwen model installed by Ollama.
