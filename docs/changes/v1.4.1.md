### What's new in 1.4.1
- **Too slow on your computer? Fixelect tells you.** If the AI model answers slowly on your computer day after day, Fixelect offers a smaller, faster model once: *"Fixes are slow on this computer"* with **Switch** and **Keep**. Switch downloads the smaller model in the background and moves to it; Keep means it never asks again.
  - It measures the engine's speed, not how long your text is, so long texts never count as slow.
  - One busy hour (an update or a game) never triggers it: it needs slow answers on at least two different days.
  - Fixelect never switches models by itself.
