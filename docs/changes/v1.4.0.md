### What's new in 1.4.0
- **More mistakes fixed.** Fixelect now catches common learner mistakes it used to leave alone: *the train already left* → *had already left*, *depend of* → *depend on*, *discussed about* → *discussed*, *I am agree* → *I agree*, *excepted* → *accepted*, and text-speak like *r u coming tonite* → *are you coming tonight*. On our hardest test texts, fixes rose from 12 to 16 out of 20 with Gemma 4.
- **Gemma 4 E2B is the new recommended model.** In our tests it fixed the most: 16 of 20 hard texts, against 10 for Qwen 2.5 3B. It also did best in other languages (18 of 18) and Uzbek, and ran as fast as the 3B, both with and without a graphics card. Qwen 2.5 1.5B stays the pick for smaller computers. Your current model is not changed; switch in Settings → Model.
- **Technical words are safe.** Words like *crashlooping* or *healthcheck* are no longer "corrected" into different words.

### Tested, not added
- **Qwen 3.5 2B and 4B (2026).** Qwen 3.5 4B is the smartest on its own, but it is 3× slower with a graphics card and takes about 10 seconds per fix without one. Once Fixelect's filter is applied, it still fixes slightly less than Gemma 4. We'll look again when the engine runs them faster.
